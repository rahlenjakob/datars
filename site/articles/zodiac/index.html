Unsolved

The Zodiac, mapped

Between December 1968 and October 1969, a killer who named himself after the stars attacked seven people around San Francisco Bay, then taunted the police for years in letters and ciphers. The case is still open.

By the datars graphics deskSept. 28, 2026

The attacks that the police attribute to the Zodiac killer took place on quiet roads, in a parking lot, by a lake and on a residential street in San Francisco, all within about 60 miles of the city. Five people died. The killer was never identified.

Four attacks in ten months

San Francisco Bay, 1968–69. The attributed attacks cluster in the North Bay, around Benicia, Vallejo and Lake Berryessa, and in the city itself.

Dec. 20, 1968. On Lake Herman Road outside Benicia, David Faraday, 17, and Betty Lou Jensen, 16, are shot in a parked car. Neither survives.

July 4, 1969. About four miles away, in the parking lot at Blue Rock Springs in Vallejo, Darlene Ferrin is killed and Michael Mageau badly wounded. A caller claims both attacks.

Sept. 27, 1969. At Lake Berryessa, a man in a black hood marked with a crossed circle ties up two students and stabs them. Cecelia Shepard dies; Bryan Hartnell survives.

Oct. 11, 1969. In Presidio Heights, San Francisco, the cabdriver Paul Stine is shot at Washington and Cherry Streets. The killer mails a piece of his bloodied shirt to The San Francisco Chronicle.

And after. In March 1970, Kathleen Johns and her baby escaped a man who had stopped her on Highway 132 near Modesto. She later said he was the Zodiac; whether he was is still disputed.

Sites are placed from public descriptions, to within a few hundred meters.

Closing in

The intervals tell their own story. Six months passed between the first attack and the second; twelve weeks between the second and the third; two between the third and the fourth. Then, as far as the police know, the killing stopped, though the letters did not.

The letters

Days after the Blue Rock Springs shooting, three Bay Area newspapers received letters, each with a third of a 408-symbol cipher and a demand to print it. They did. Most of the letters arrived in 1969 and 1970, a few more in 1974; how many were genuine is still argued over.

"This is the Zodiac speaking": the opening of many of the letters, and the name he gave himself.

Two ciphers, 51 years apart

The 408-symbol cipher was solved within a week, in August 1969, by Donald and Bettye Harden, a schoolteacher and his wife in Salinas. It did not reveal his name. A second, 340-symbol cipher resisted amateurs, cryptographers and the F.B.I. until December 2020, when David Oranchak, Sam Blake and Jarl Van Eycke cracked it with a mix of software and patience.

The 340 did not name him either. The San Francisco Police Department's case remains open. The best-known suspect, Arthur Leigh Allen, was never charged and died in 1992.
