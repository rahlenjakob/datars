Features

Everything a chart needs, from one document

datars is an engine and a standard library. You describe a chart — its data, its marks, its states — and the engine does the rest on every platform: layout, text, motion, interaction, accessibility, delivery. Each of the pages below goes into one part of that, with live charts and real numbers.
