The documents behind the gallery

A chart is a TypeScript file: data, a scene of recipes and a program of steps. Here is each gallery chart's, as it was built for this site, with the theme tokens it reads and what each tier of its bundle weighs — the lab in the gallery shows the same for the chart you open.
